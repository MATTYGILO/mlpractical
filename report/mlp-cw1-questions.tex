\youranswer{

    Question 6 - Explain how one could use a combination of L1 and L2 regularisation. Discuss any potential benefits of this approach. Present an equation for this combined loss function and explain all relevant variables and hyperparameters.}

    L1 regularisation adds a term which is the scaled sum of the given weights.
    As such, during back propagation, the optimiser will try to decrease the general loss whilst
    ensuring that the overal size of the weights are minimised. This can have the effect of increasing sparsity,
    as the optimiser will learn to put the majority of the strength of the weights in the most essential connections.

    L2 regularisation adds a term which is a scaled sum of the squares of the weights,
    this leads to larger weights being accentuated more than smaller weights. As such the optimiser will incentived
    to reduce the larger weights more than the smaller weights.
    This can improve generalisation as probabilties can be distributed more evenly across the different weights.

    L1 and L2 can be used together to provide a balance between increasing sparsity and increasing spread amognst the weights.
    By weighting their parameters differently, we can prioritise different regularisation over others.


}
